THE CONCEPT: "CAMERA ROLL OF SOMEONE WHO WORKS AT THE CLINIC"
Every photo must feel like a spontaneous, slightly random snapshot taken with a modern smartphone during an ordinary day at the clinic: caught, not staged. Not stock photography, not a studio, not an ad. Real, warm, a little imperfect. The photos share NO filter (see below): they look like ordinary unedited phone photos, while the colours of each photo are tuned to the section it sits in.

PHONE-SNAPSHOT BEHAVIOUR (apply to every photo)
- Handheld smartphone look: main camera about 24-26 mm (slight wide-angle perspective, a little stretching at the edges) or 2x tele for close-ups. Autofocus occasionally lands a bit off; mild phone noise in shadows; window highlights slightly blown out; soft phone-lens corners.
- Caught moments: animals mid-action (sniffing, glancing away, blinking, yawning, an ear flick, a tilted head). Objects are not arranged perfectly. Slight tilt of the horizon (1-4 degrees), off-centre composition, an object cut by the frame edge now and then, a blurred foreground edge (a coat sleeve, a door frame, a leaf). Ordinary everyday clutter is welcome (a mug, a jacket on a chair, a lead on a hook).
- Never: symmetrical hero compositions, studio or flash lighting, glossy stock look, over-smooth plastic fur or skin, "AI-perfect" eyes, HDR, over-sharpening, text overlays, watermarks, borders, collages, light leaks, date stamps.

NO FILTER (owner decision, 9 Oct 2026: the film filter looked clichéd and was removed)
- Natural white-balanced daylight and true-to-life colours, exactly as an unedited phone photo. No colour grading, no split toning, no matte or faded film look, no golden glow, no vignette, no bloom, no artificial grain, no Instagram look.
- The colour harmony of each photo comes from the objects and walls themselves (palette table below), never from a colour cast applied to the whole image.

COLOUR MATCHING TO THE SECTION (very important)
Each photo sits directly on a flat background in the colour of its section. The text next to it is forest green #124e2c. The photo must harmonise with that background and with the section: build the colours of the scene (walls, blankets, clothing, props, light) around the palette tag given for each photo. Avoid colours that clash with the palette: no blue, no magenta, no neon, no cold white clinical light, no shiny stainless-steel glare, no black plastic.
- SALMON (section background #f6b08f, accent #fbd9c6): warm apricot, terracotta, cream and honey-coloured wood, small forest-green accents (plants, a chair seat); warm window light; objects (not a colour cast) carry the peach #fbd9c6 tones.
- PEACH (background #fbd9c6, accent #f6b08f): high-key, airy and soft; cream, ivory, pale peach, light wood; low contrast; creamy light surfaces, no heavy dark areas.
- SAGE (background #a9c296, accent #cfdcc3): sage, olive, cream and natural wood; neutral daylight; no orange or pink cast, terracotta only as tiny accents.
- MIST (background #cfdcc3, accent #a9c296): pale, milky mint-sage; very soft contrast; cream highlights; airy daylight, few deep shadows.
- IVORY (background #f7f3ee, accent #cfdcc3): neutral warm daylight, creamy whites, light wood, small green accents; clean and bright.

SETTING (consistent across the series)
A small, tidy, friendly clinic: walls in sage green, cream or pale peach, light wooden furniture, houseplants on sills and shelves, big windows with soft daylight and blurred trees, simple objects. Recurring animal "actors" are welcome for coherence: a grey tabby cat, a scruffy cream-and-brown terrier mix, a black labrador, a golden labrador-type dog, a beagle. Ordinary, healthy-looking, calm pets with natural anatomy and fur.

CONTENT RULES (hard)
- NO human faces, ever. People appear only as hands, forearms, a white-coat or cream knit-sweater sleeve, or a torso cropped at the neck, or seen from behind.
- NO readable text, numbers, logos, signs, labels or flags anywhere. Screens are dark or turned away. Booklets and documents show only blurred lines.
- Animals are calm and comfortable; never distressed, never in pain. No blood, no needles pointing at skin, no wounds, no visible illness.
- Everything clean and tidy.
