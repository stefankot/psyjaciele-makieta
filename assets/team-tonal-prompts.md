## Wspólny prompt

Use case: identity-preserve, lighting retouch.
Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET: an existing real veterinary team member cutout. Image 2 is ONLY a tonal/lighting reference of a dog; it is displayed in grayscale on the site. Edit ONLY image 1, never include the dog.
Produce a very natural premium editorial studio portrait with Vogue-inspired photographic finish. Pure neutral black-and-white, bright luminous midtones, soft silver-gray highlights, gently lifted blacks and rich but restrained contrast, as in the grayscale dog reference. Retouch uneven lighting and photographic noise carefully, preserve real skin pores and texture, natural age and facial features. Professional soft large studio light and subtle fill, restrained detailed sharpness with no artificial halos.
Lighten dark clothing clearly to a medium charcoal/silver-gray with visible fabric detail and folds rather than near-black. Lift dark hair slightly, preserving its original dark character and individual strands. Keep light clothing naturally light with highlight detail.
STRICT INVARIANTS: exact same person, face, expression, facial proportions, eyes, age, hairstyle, pose, body silhouette, fingers, hands, arms, clothing design, jewelry and medical equipment. No new makeup, no beautification reshaping, no face swap, no reconstructed body. Preserve the original canvas aspect ratio, subject scale, head position, framing and all transparent margins so it fits the existing crop on the website. No crop or zoom. Output a precisely cut out PNG with genuine transparent background and fine clean hair edges, no visible backdrop, no drop shadow, no text.
